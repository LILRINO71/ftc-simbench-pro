Support

Last updated 14 November 2026

FTC SimBench Pro is free. Help comes from the student who builds it, so please give us a few days to answer.

Report a bug or ask a question

Open an issue on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Please include:

  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
  • how you brought your robot in: the pasted Onshape link, an export zip, or a STEP file;
  • your browser and computer (for example, Chrome on a school Chromebook);
  • a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Please don't post your Onshape link in a public issue unless your document is already public. Describe the robot instead, or say so and we'll find a private way to look at it.

See the existing issues first: your question may already be answered.

Common questions

My robot won't load from the Onshape link

  • Paste the address of the Assembly tab, not a Part Studio.
  • When Onshape asks, click Allow. SimBench can only read your documents; it can never change them.
  • If your school blocks pop-ups or third-party sites, ask for ftc-simbench-pro.pages.dev and cdn.jsdelivr.net to be allowed.
  • Still stuck? Export the assembly instead (right-click the Assembly tab → Export → URDF, GLB, Medium) and drop the zip on the page.

A part moves wrong, or doesn't move

Every moving part needs a mate in Onshape (revolute, slider, cylindrical), with limits on the ones that are mechanisms. The Sim-ready CAD checklist explains it in a few minutes.

Where is my robot kept?

In your own browser, so it is there next time. ⋮ → Forget my robot removes it. See Privacy.

Can I revoke SimBench's access to Onshape?

Yes, at any time: in Onshape, your account icon → My account → Applications, then revoke FTC SimBench. You can also sign out in SimBench's import card.
