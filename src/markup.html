            Driving

            • Drag the robot on the field to move it. Shift-drag or right-drag turns it.
            • Plug in a controller and press a button: it becomes gamepad1 or gamepad2.
            • Keys IJKL drive, UO turn, ABXY, QE bumpers, arrows d-pad, F fire. Each key goes to the gamepad your code reads it from.
