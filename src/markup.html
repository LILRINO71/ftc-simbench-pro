    0.90
    
        
    
        
    
        
    Rigid body mode drives the robot with its real mass, centre of mass and wheel traction taken from the
          CAD: it accelerates like the build, transfers load when you brake, and breaks grip if you ask for more than the
          tiles can give. Kinematic mode is the old behaviour — instant, perfect, and wrong.
