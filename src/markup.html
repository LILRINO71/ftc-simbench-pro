            Get your robot from Onshape
            
        
            Every part, its colour and weight, and every joint from your mates, from your Onshape assembly. The sure way is a file: Onshape exports it and you drop it here. It works on school computers, with school accounts, in any browser.
            
        
            This needs a computer: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari or a Chromebook.
            

        
            
          
              
            
            1. 
              1
              
              
                
              In Onshape, right-click your Assembly tab and choose Export…
              
                
              The tabs run along the bottom of the Onshape window. It has to be the assembly, not a Part Studio: the joints live in the assembly.
              
                
                Open Onshape in a new tab ↗
              
              
            
              2. 
            
            2. 
              2
              
              
                
              Set Format to URDF and Geometry to STL, then click Export
              
                
              Leave everything else as it is. Onshape builds a .zip and your browser downloads it. Don't unzip it. If the dialog offers a mesh resolution, pick the coarsest: a robot's hardware has thousands of holes, and SimBench simplifies the shapes anyway.
              
                
              
              
            
              3. 
            
            3. 
              3
              
              
                
              Drop that .zip on SimBench
              
                
              or drag it from your downloads onto the robot box
              
                
              Your mates arrive as joints with their limits, gear relations as couplings, and every part with its name, colour and weight. SimBench keeps the robot in this browser for next time.
              
                
              Zero questions: in Onshape, rename the mate each motor or servo drives to that device's name in your code (spinner, outtake, uppies…). The export carries the names, and those joints bind themselves. Every other revolute mate is treated as a bearing that just spins.
              
              
              
            
              4. 
          
            
          
            No URDF in the Format list? Make sure you right-clicked the Assembly tab. If it's still missing, export GLTF or STEP instead: the shapes come in, and the joints are found from the geometry and asked about.

            It didn't work?
          
            
            
            The Onshape window didn't open
            
            
            Your browser blocked the pop-up. Click Sign in with Onshape again: this time it opens in this tab and brings you back here.
            
            
            Onshape asked me to sign in, then nothing happened
            
            
            Finish signing in and click Allow. If the window is gone, click Sign in with Onshape again.
            
            
            It says the link isn't an assembly
            
            
            The address has to come from the Assembly tab, not a Part Studio or a drawing. Click that tab in Onshape, then copy the address again.
            
            
            It says I'm not allowed (401 or 403)
            
            
            The document has to be yours or shared with you (view access is enough). Click sign out, then sign in again with the Onshape account that owns the robot.
            
            
            Export doesn't offer URDF
            
            
            You're exporting a Part Studio or a drawing. Right-click the Assembly tab along the bottom of the Onshape window and choose Export there.
            
            
            SimBench says there's no robot in the zip
            
            
            Look inside it for a urdf folder with a .urdf file and a meshes folder beside it. If it holds only STL files, export again with Format set to URDF.
            
            
            Some parts are missing
            
            
            Parts hidden or suppressed in the assembly aren't exported. A part with no material in Onshape still comes in, weighed by its shape.
            
            
            The bookmark does nothing, or I see about:blank#blocked
            
            
            Your browser blocks bookmarks that run code, and dropping the button on the Onshape page never works. Use the export instead.
            
            
            It's taking a long time
            
            
            Every part studio is read once; a robot with 100 of them takes a minute or two.
